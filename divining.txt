Gentlemen, today I will tell you your future.
This little Earth we are on is located in an ordinary solar system, revolving around an average-level star on the edge of the Milky Way. But what is going to happen next in this solar system, in this galaxy, in this universe, is quite wonderful.
Let's go thousands to hundreds of thousands of years into the future. You have already conquered the moons of Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn, and various places in our solar system. You have already sent interstellar automated probes outside the solar system. Even though the different countries that still exist on Earth try to take control of various places in the solar system, your people there somehow try to establish their own governments. The first off-world war, born from a minor disagreement between Moon-dwellers and Earth-dwellers, ended a while ago. Among the many children born in Shackleton City on the moon, the first child to Sri Lankan parents born off-Earth is also born. But that child does not belong to Sri Lanka; they belong to the sky.
Now we are going a million to 100 million years into the future. You have spread everywhere in the Milky Way. Although your people in various corners of the galaxy call themselves humans, they have modified themselves so much to survive on different worlds under different conditions that they can hardly recognize each other as human anymore. The debate over whether artificially altered humans are truly human becomes fierce at this stage. Even though you feel humanity is distancing itself from its humanity, your children still take a box of crayons and draw the same scribbles a stone-age child drew. Because of that, you sometimes feel that humans haven't changed all that much.
As a billion years pass, the temperature of your sun gradually increases, turning Earth into a desert world naturally incapable of sustaining life. Through the acceleration of your world's water cycle and the separation of hydrogen and oxygen by the power of solar rays, Earth's water slowly escapes into space. To your people spread across the galaxy, Earth is just a historical tale, much like a house left behind when it gets old. Some still mourn that you didn't maintain Earth as it was, even though you had the ability to do so. But your focus has always been your expansion, your people's progress. As you advanced, you never once looked back. And Earth, by this time, is just one waystation in your past. Some of your people have now completely separated from biological matter; they live only inside non-living materials you created. Others still live in biological frames that are different from their human bodies in many ways, yet similar in some.
The next milestone in your cosmic journey is the 'death' of the sun. After 5 billion years, the hydrogen gas on the sun runs out, and the helium within begins to undergo nuclear fusion. With the start of helium fusion, the core pushes the outer layers outward, and the sun expands to become a red giant. The sun, which gave light, warmth, and life to Earth for billions of years, arrives like a giant reaper and swallows your ancient home. To your people spread across the galaxy, and even beyond, this is just a single sentence in a news broadcast. But some of your people observe a minute of silence, remembering the mother world of your species. Earth and the solar system are just a picture hanging on the museum walls of your people, who are still on their cosmic journey. Your children ask you what it is. You try to explain the origin of humanity that happened in that solar system, but you feel you cannot do it justice.
Let us travel to the future between 20 and 50 billion years from now. Due to the unstoppable expansion of the universe, all galaxies except those in your 'cosmic village' move out of your observable horizon. Bound by the physical laws of the speed of light, you cannot leave the galaxy cluster you are in now. Apart from the Milky Way, Andromeda, the Large Magellanic Cloud, and a few other small star groups, you can only travel into the unfathomable dark of the universe. Yet, even though this is a relatively small village, it is unimaginably large; you have all the space you could ever want. Having known about this event for billions of years, it is not a terrifying occurrence to you. There is plenty of space in this cosmic village for your people to spread and for their future children to live.
After a hundred billion years, your universe has become a dark and quiet place. The creation of new stars that had been happening in the universe since its beginning has completely stopped. But just because new stars aren't formed, the death of existing stars doesn't stop. Therefore, you, whose survival depends on stars, have to extract energy from the remaining ones. Your people have now become brilliant stellar engineers.Even though you don't have the ability to create stars, you are now skilled at getting the maximum use out of them. You know that the end of the universe will arrive someday. Even though you are beings completely separated from matter by this time, you cannot escape the need for matter and energy. However, you are completely separated from flesh bodies, genes, and childbirth. Regardless, in the stories your 'children' tell while looking at the stars, in the games they play, the wonder you held in the distant past and the desire to go on long journeys still remains.
In the distant past, your people, roaming in groups in the dark forests of your world, lit campfires to try to chase away the darkness. Your life depended on that campfire. A trillion years later, as if caught in a cruel joke of destiny, you have to keep your species alive by gathering around the only remaining type of stars in the universe: red dwarfs. From the countless, diverse stars that once existed in the universe, now you are left only with these slowly burning stars. Living in this gradually darkening universe, you still tell each other stories that are not entirely true, still create music, you still shout out to the universe that you are here. Your technological advancement is unimaginably profound, advanced enough to defy the physical laws of the universe. Even after a trillion years, though the river of humanity still exists, it flows slowly, but the river still flows.
As a hundred trillion years pass, even the last stars in the universe die out and become black dwarves. You get your energy from dead stars. But as you have already separated from matter and your physical shells, the amount of energy you need decreases. It takes you thousands of years just to complete a single thought. You are in no rush to go anywhere. Now there is only existence.
Leaving physical matter completely behind and passing trillions, quadrillions of years, the era of proton decay begins. I truly do not have words to tell you what kind of state you are in now, what kind of people you are. As more time passes, the only energy-producing units in the universe are black holes. The darkest era of your time is now beginning. Knowing about the total halt of the universe and the endless darkness coming in the future, you search for something you can do about it. Some of your people think searching for existence even after the end of the universe is foolishness. What is the point of trying to live in a universe where there is absolutely nothing?
Black holes, through Hawking radiation, decay very slowly over millions of years. The only usable energy left in the entire universe comes solely from this radiation. Yet you are clever enough to use even that. Like small candles in the thick dark, your life still goes on around the black holes. Before the final death of the universe is destined to happen - before the final black hole is destroyed - you discover the ability to create energy and matter. Using this knowledge, you can create a brand new universe! Having gained the ability to create a universe, are you God now? Was the universe you were in for so long also created like this? Even if these questions are in the corner of your mind, you just did what you always did: you used your knowledge and solved a problem. Are you God to the other beings living in the universe you built for yourself? No matter how far you have strayed from humanity now, according to you, you haven't achieved 'godhood.'
What will your future be like, going to live in a new universe you created? Do you start from the beginning? Will you exist as an invisible force in the new universe? Your story doesn't end here. Gentlemen! This is just another beginning for you."
